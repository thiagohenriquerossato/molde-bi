# Phase 6 Plan 02: Gráficos ECharts do Financeiro

<objective>
Adicionar `renderFinanceCharts(containers, contas, theme)` ao `MoldeCharts` com os 10 gráficos do Financeiro, incluindo heatmap mês×dia e curva ABC (Pareto top 15). Reusar tema, cores e `dispose` do padrão da Fase 5.
</objective>

<must_haves>
- `renderFinanceCharts` instancia até 10 gráficos e retorna instâncias para dispose
- Heatmap usa `visualMap` + `series.type = "heatmap"`
- ABC combina barras + linha % acumulado (segundo yAxis 0–100)
- Cores de `--chart-*`, tooltips pt-BR, locale currency
- Nenhum dado de pedidos/receita
</must_haves>

## 1. renderFinanceCharts

<task>
id: `06-02-01`
type: `feature`
files:

<read_first>

- `.planning/phases/06-financeiro-e-contas-a-pagar/06-UI-SPEC.md`
</read_first>

<action>
Adicionar `renderFinanceCharts(containers, contas, theme)` a `MoldeCharts`, reusando `getChartColors`, `formatBrl`, `formatMonthLabel`, `initChart`, `baseTooltip`.

Containers (keys): `expensesMonth`, `paidOpenMonth`, `expensesCategory`, `expensesClassification`, `topSuppliers`, `dueHeatmap`, `supplierAbc`, `fixedEvolution`, `variableEvolution`, `bankAccount`.

Séries via `MoldeMetrics`:
1. `expensesMonth` — line: `aggregateExpensesByMonth` (x=mês, y=valor).
2. `paidOpenMonth` — bar stacked: `aggregatePaidOpenByMonth` (Pago, Aberto).
3. `expensesCategory` — pie rosca (`radius: ["40%","70%"]`): `expensesByCategory`.
4. `expensesClassification` — bar horizontal: `expensesByClassification` (reverse arrays).
5. `topSuppliers` — bar horizontal: `topSuppliers` (limit 15, reverse).
6. `dueHeatmap` — `dueHeatmapMatrix`: xAxis category dias `1..31`, yAxis category meses (`formatMonthLabel`), `visualMap` contínuo (min 0, max = maior valor), `series.type = "heatmap"`, tooltip currency.
7. `supplierAbc` — `supplierAbc`: xAxis category labels (+ "Outros"), yAxis[0] valor, yAxis[1] percentual (0–100, `axisLabel formatter "{value}%"`), series bar (valor) + line (acumuladoPct no yAxisIndex 1).
8. `fixedEvolution` — line: `aggregateFixedVariableByMonth` série `fixa`.
9. `variableEvolution` — line: `aggregateFixedVariableByMonth` série `variavel`.
10. `bankAccount` — bar: `expensesByBankAccount`.

Todos com `aria.enabled`, `animationDuration: 300`, grid adequado, tooltip currency. Retornar `instances.filter(Boolean)`.
</action>

<acceptance_criteria>

- `rg "supplierAbc|acumuladoPct|yAxisIndex" js/charts.js`.
- `node --check js/charts.js` exit 0.
</acceptance_criteria>

<verify>
